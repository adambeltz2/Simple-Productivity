Dropbox is optional

Notebook works fully offline in this browser. Connecting Dropbox adds an encrypted backup that you can restore from on another device — it never replaces this browser's storage as the source of truth.

Without Dropbox
  • Notes stay in this browser
  • Nothing leaves your machine
  • No account needed
With Dropbox
  • Backed up automatically
  • Restorable on another device
  • You can disconnect anytime
